公司簡介 · About Us

助霖工業有限公司長期服務國內外市場，累積深厚製造經驗，並隨著產業與世代變遷， 逐步導入智慧化與 AI 應用，持續提供穩定且具競爭力的產品。

With decades of manufacturing expertise, Ji Lin Industrial continuously adapts to industrial transformation by integrating intelligent and AI-driven solutions, delivering reliable and competitive products worldwide.

2020s
